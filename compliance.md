# Compliance Guardrails

This skill is a strategy framework, not a substitute for current marketplace policy or legal review.

## Never invent

- sales volume
- number of buyers
- ratings
- awards
- certifications
- laboratory results
- test data
- “official” status
- medical or health claims
- country of origin
- warranties
- return terms
- punishment / compensation promises
- competitor performance

## Comparison rules

A comparison should be:
- relevant
- fair
- based on comparable conditions
- supported by evidence
- not deceptively edited

## Numeric claims

Record the source or evidence for every important number.

If evidence is missing:
- write “evidence needed”
- use a non-numeric concept version
- do not fabricate a placeholder as if it were real

## Platform policy

When the user asks whether a specific phrase, claim, image, or promotion is permitted on a current marketplace, verify the current marketplace rules before giving a definitive answer.

## Sensitive categories

Food, mother-and-baby, cosmetics, health-related products, supplements, protective equipment, and regulated goods can carry extra claim restrictions. Treat those claims conservatively and require stronger evidence.
