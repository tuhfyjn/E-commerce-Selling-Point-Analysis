# Desk Fan Example

## Input

- Product: desktop circulation fan
- Category: small appliance / fan
- Target users: dormitory, office, bedroom
- Features: 3 speeds, quiet operation, circulation airflow, adjustable angle
- Pains: weak airflow, noisy at night, narrow airflow direction
- Concerns: whether quietness is real; whether airflow is enough
- Evidence: noise test pending; wind-speed test pending
- After-sales: 7-day return; 365-day replacement policy

## Diagnosis

- Click barrier: looks similar to dozens of ordinary desk fans.
- Purchase barrier: user does not know whether “quiet + strong airflow” is actually credible.

## Purchase-reason translation

1. Adjustable circulation airflow → wider room coverage → less need to aim the fan directly.
2. Low-noise design → less disturbance at night → suitable for desk/bedside use.
3. Compact desk form → easy placement → useful in dorm, bedroom, and office.

## Primary hook

**Benefit / scenario hybrid**

Headline concept:

> 不只吹一个方向，桌面也能循环送风

Evidence needed:
- airflow coverage demonstration
- verified noise data

## Five-image plan

1. Click: fan + wide-airflow visual; one headline.
2. Selling point: circulation + quiet + adjustable angle, all tied to “small-space comfort”.
3. Detail: grille, motor/air duct, adjustment structure.
4. Specification/scenario: dimensions, desk footprint, bedroom/office/dorm.
5. Guarantee: real return/warranty terms.

## A/B test

- A: pain hook — “普通小风扇只吹一个方向”
- B: benefit hook — “桌面也能循环送风”
- Primary metric: CTR
