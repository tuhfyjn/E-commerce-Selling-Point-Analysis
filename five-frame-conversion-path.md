# Five-Frame Conversion Path

## Frame 1 — Entry

Question: What is this and why should I click?

Job:
- instant product recognition
- one dominant reason
- category-appropriate visual impact

## Frame 2 — Reason

Question: Why this one?

Job:
- strongest purchase logic
- 2–4 supporting sub-reasons
- clear differentiation

## Frame 3 — Proof

Question: Is it really good?

Job:
- detail, test, structure, scene, or comparison proof
- turn quality claims into observable evidence

## Frame 4 — Fit

Question: Is it right for me?

Job:
- size
- weight
- capacity
- compatibility
- use scenarios
- variants
- target-user fit

## Frame 5 — Safety

Question: What if something goes wrong?

Job:
- return
- warranty
- authenticity
- packaging
- service
- shipping
- certification

## Important

The sequence follows decision logic, not a fixed poster template.
