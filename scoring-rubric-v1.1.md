# 100-Point Scoring Rubric V1.1

## 1. Instant clarity — 12
- product identity obvious
- thumbnail readable

## 2. Friction targeting — 12
- addresses the dominant click/purchase barrier

## 3. Purchase reason — 16
- feature translated into concrete user value

## 4. Differentiation — 12
- memorable and not generic

## 5. Visual proof — 16
- image proves the claim
- No-Copy Proof Test passes

## 6. Product fidelity — 12
- geometry/components/materials remain truthful

## 7. Information hierarchy — 8
- one dominant visual boss
- low clutter

## 8. Gallery rhythm — 5
- set is not repetitive
- roles are visually distinct

## 9. Trust / compliance — 7
- claims supported
- platform constraints respected

### Interpretation
- 90–100: strong A/B-test candidate
- 85–89: ready with minor refinements
- 75–84: strategically useful but still weak in one or two layers
- 60–74: mostly descriptive imagery
- below 60: rebuild the decision logic
