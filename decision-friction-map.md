# Decision Friction Map

The key question is not “what can we say?” but “what prevents the next decision?”

## 1. Click friction

Why does the user not enter?

Common causes:
- product identity unclear
- no immediate value
- no contrast against competitors
- product too small
- image too busy
- hook too abstract
- category visual language mismatched

## 2. Purchase friction

Why does the user not buy after entering?

Common causes:
- unclear reason to choose this SKU
- weak evidence
- compatibility uncertainty
- size/fit uncertainty
- quality concern
- authenticity concern
- service concern
- perceived price-value mismatch

## 3. Four friction types

### Need friction
The user does not feel urgency or relevance.

### Difference friction
The product feels interchangeable.

### Proof friction
The claim is not credible enough.

### Risk friction
The user fears the consequences of a wrong purchase.
