# Consistency and Style

## 1. Product Identity Guard

Across the set, keep:
- silhouette
- proportions
- component positions
- brand marks
- material logic
- color logic

## 2. Reference Style Genome

Extract reusable visual variables:

- lighting behavior
- contrast
- color temperature
- typography attitude
- information density
- composition tension
- product occupancy
- background complexity
- commercial mood

Do not copy:
- exact scene
- exact graphic layout
- unique brand assets
- distinctive creative expression

## 3. Gallery Rhythm Check

A strong set has a visual cadence.

Default target for 5 images:
- at least 4 distinct dominant visual structures
- at least one scene or proof action
- at least one detail/structural frame
- at least one fit/specification frame
- at least one trust/service frame

Avoid five near-identical hero posters.
